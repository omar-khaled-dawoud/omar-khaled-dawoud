## About me

I'm a Teaching Assistant at the Faculty of Computers and Informatics, Tanta University, and an M.Sc. student at Zagazig University, working on machine learning and optimization.

Much of research turns out to be data work: handling imbalanced data, building reproducible experiment pipelines, and keeping every result traceable. That is the part I enjoy most, and it is pulling me toward data engineering for AI.

## Research

> **Machine learning models for smart grid stability prediction: a comparative analysis**
> *Scientific Reports*, 2026 · [doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47385-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-47385-x)

Benchmarked 14 machine learning models for smart grid stability prediction, compared Bayesian (TPE) and metaheuristic (Grey Wolf Optimizer) hyperparameter optimization, and used explainable AI (SHAP, PDP, ICE) to keep the models transparent.
